AI & Transformation

Where AI projects actually fail in telco

Most telco AI initiatives stall in the same places: poor data quality from decades of legacy BSS/OSS, integration complexity that vendors downplay, and a gap between impressive PoC demos and production systems that actually handle edge cases at scale. I've seen this first-hand - and it's what I build around.

Agentic AI for Telcos

Building and deploying agentic AI prototypes for telco use cases - billing dispute resolution, customer journey automation, call centre triage. The hard part isn't the LLM. It's the system integration, the error handling, and earning trust from operations teams who've been burned by automation before.

Decision Intelligence

AI systems that provide auditable, explainable recommendations for complex commercial decisions - traffic steering, pricing optimisation, risk assessment. In regulated telco environments, a black-box recommendation is worse than no recommendation at all.

Data Infrastructure

Architected real-time analytics (Apache Pinot, Superset) powering automated billing reconciliation for 300K+ managed SIMs. Getting value from AI starts with getting your data house in order - most operators aren't there yet.

API-First Platform Design

Led architecture of an IoT CMP with full programmatic control. Without clean APIs, you can't integrate AI/ML into operations - you just end up with another dashboard nobody looks at.
